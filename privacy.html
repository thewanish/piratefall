Privacy policy

This policy explains how Piratefall: Battle Monsters handles information. For privacy questions or requests, contact shilstorefront@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

The game stores journeys, settings, inventory, coins, diamonds, companions, blueprints and purchase delivery records on your device. This information makes it possible to continue playing and deliver rewards. The game does not require a separate Piratefall account.

iCloud saves

When iCloud saving is available and enabled, eligible progress is saved in the app container associated with your Apple account. This can include journeys, coins, diamonds, owned companions, blueprints and other saved unlocks. Apple operates iCloud. We do not receive your Apple account password.

Cloud recovery depends on a completed upload, the same Apple account, available storage and iCloud access. You can manage the app's cloud data through your Apple device settings. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete its iCloud data.

Optional purchases

Apple processes purchases in the iPhone app. The game uses product identifiers, verified transaction identifiers and purchase status to deliver items, avoid duplicate delivery and recover eligible purchases. We do not receive payment card details. Purchase and ownership records may be included in your saved progress. Apple retains its own transaction records under its policies.

Local network play

Local cooperative play exchanges room details, player names, connection addresses, access credentials and gameplay updates between participating devices on the same network. The host device runs the room. These details are used to find rooms, connect players and synchronize play. This feature does not upload gameplay to a developer operated multiplayer server.

Room credentials and connection details may be saved on your device so you can reconnect. Other participants can see information needed to play together, such as your chosen player name and character actions.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate problems. Please avoid sending sensitive information. Support email is handled through Gmail.

Advertising and tracking

The current game does not include advertising networks or third party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this support website. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. This site does not add analytics scripts, advertising cookies or contact forms. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Service providers

Apple provides the App Store, purchase processing and iCloud. Google provides support email through Gmail. GitHub provides this website. Those providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and control

Device saves remain until removed through the game, device settings or uninstalling. iCloud data remains until you delete it or Apple removes it under its service rules. We keep support correspondence while needed to resolve requests and maintain relevant support records. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any information we must retain for legal reasons.

We cannot directly access or erase your private iCloud account. Manage cloud data through Apple settings, or contact us for guidance. Apple controls its purchase records.

Younger players

The game does not request a child's name, birth date or contact information to play. Parents and guardians can manage purchases, local network permissions and iCloud settings on their device. If a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to request its removal.

International access and changes

Our service providers may process information in other countries under their own policies. Core game data handling is the same across regions. Store prices and product availability can vary by Apple storefront. We will update this page if our practices change and show the effective date above.
